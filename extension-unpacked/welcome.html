Welcome to HistoryOut. Here is a useful first step, without an account or any setup.

Open HistoryOut →Open in a full tab

Open beside this page, or use the full-tab workspace.

Keep its familiar blue icon within reach.
Open your browser’s extensions menu, find HistoryOut, and pin it to the toolbar. Click the blue icon whenever you need it.

  1. Take a look at today. Choose Today and select Preview. Your history is read only when you ask.
  2. Find one useful page. Search a word from its title or URL, or pick a top website. Open the link to return to it.
  3. Keep a file when you need one. Choose CSV for a spreadsheet, JSON for data, or HTML for a readable list of links.
Try Yesterday tomorrow.

It is a quick way to pick up research again. Save a view if you often use the same search or website.
