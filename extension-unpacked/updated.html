More reliable exports and saved settings.

  • More complete exports. Matching visits are no longer missed if you reopen a page while HistoryOut is loading your history.
  • Clearer website counts. A website’s visit count now matches the visits included by its website filter.
  • Saved searches stay exact. Saved views keep your full search text, including long URLs, so reopening them returns the same matches.
  • Clearer saved dates. Date ranges saved in older versions now show their exact start and end times in your local time, so it is clear which visits will be exported.
  • Your latest choices stay saved. Your date range, file format and column choices are saved even if you close HistoryOut straight after changing them.

Version 2.0.0: a little more useful, every day

Welcome back to HistoryOut.
Your existing export preferences stay with you. Everything is still free, with no additional permissions.

Added

A daily look back.

Choose Today or Yesterday, then Preview. See your visits, pages and top websites, and reopen a useful page straight from the trail.

Added

A shortcut to your research.

Search titles and URLs, filter by website, then save a named view. Next time, apply it and Preview to pick up the thread.

Improved

More control over your file.

Preview matching rows, keep one row per URL, or remove query strings and fragments. Choose your fields and export CSV, JSON or clickable HTML.

Refined

A calmer workspace.

See the full change list

Added

  • Today and Yesterday date presets with a recap of visits, unique pages and websites.
  • A recent trail with safe clickable links and title/URL search.
  • Website filtering, including subdomains, and one-click top website filters.
  • Up to 12 locally saved views for repeatable searches and export settings.
  • Preview before export, optional one row per URL and optional URL cleanup.
  • Optional timestamp and website columns.
  • Tell a friend with a copy confirmation and a fallback link if copying is unavailable.
  • Welcome and update pages, shown once per installed version.

Changed

  • A new layout, with the original HistoryOut logo and icon.
  • A browser-neutral workspace with a full-page fallback.
  • Clearer optional support through Buy Me a Coffee.

Fixed

  • Custom dates use local calendar boundaries.
  • History searches include pages revisited after the selected date range.
  • Safer spreadsheet text and escaped, self-contained HTML output.
  • Bounded history-read concurrency, progress, cancellation and clear failure states.

Kept

CSV, JSON and HTML export. Your valid existing preferences. Local history processing. The same permissions. A completely free extension.
