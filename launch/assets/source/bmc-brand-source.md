# Buy Me a Coffee button artwork

The local cup SVG comes unchanged from the official Buy Me a Coffee brand kit, retrieved on 5 September 2026.

- Official source: https://buymeacoffee.com/brand
- Download linked by that page: https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fo/sn103qa0af6ouffjn9xc7/AHhWrm3dpC1rNSzlapqjbOE?rlkey=36018g7kb0fi6jg7xuxtux3ke&dl=0

- Extension copy: `extension-unpacked/assets/bmc-cup.svg`
- Official full-button reference: `bmc-official-button.svg`

The extension bundles the cup locally and links to the creator's existing contribution page. It does not load the Buy Me a Coffee widget, remote images or tracking scripts.
