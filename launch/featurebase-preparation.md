## Pinned welcome post

Title: **Start here: help shape HistoryOut**

Hi, I’m Saulius, the developer of HistoryOut.

This is the place to suggest a feature, add context to an existing request, or vote on an idea you would use. HistoryOut is a free tool for exporting and revisiting the history your browser makes available. Your examples help me understand where it could work better.

A useful suggestion tells me:

1. What you were trying to do.
2. What made it difficult, or what is missing.
3. What a better result would look like.

Please search for a similar request first. If it already exists, add your vote and explain how it would help you. One idea per post keeps the conversation easy to follow.

Votes help show interest. They do not promise a release date or guarantee that a feature will be built.

This board is public. Please leave browsing history, private URLs, export files and personal information out of posts. HistoryOut does not attach any of those details when it opens this board.

Thanks for helping make a small tool more useful.

Saulius

## Pinned help post

Title: **Something not working? Here’s how to get help**

If something in HistoryOut is confusing or fails, a short description is enough to get started.

Please include your browser and its version, your HistoryOut version, the steps you tried, and what you expected to happen. If there is an error message, copy its text after removing any private details.

An example using fictional pages is more useful than a real history export. Please do not post browsing records, private links or screenshots containing personal information here.

For private help, email **sauliusthedev@gmail.com**. Please do not attach a history export; describe the problem first.

HistoryOut can read only the history your current browser profile still exposes. It cannot recover deleted history. The history limits guide explains more: https://exportchromehistory.app/guides/browser-history-limits/
